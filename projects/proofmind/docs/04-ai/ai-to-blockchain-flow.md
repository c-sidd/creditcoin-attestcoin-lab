# AI to Blockchain Flow

```text
Verified evidence
      ↓
AI context builder
      ↓
AI structured decision
      ↓
Schema validator
      ↓
Deterministic policy engine
      ↓
Transaction intent
      ↓
Executor checks chain state + nonce + limits
      ↓
Creditcoin contract
      ↓
Execution event
      ↓
Evidence record
```

The model never becomes the final authorization layer. The chain transaction must be valid even if the AI provider is unavailable, replaced or compromised.
